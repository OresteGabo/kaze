# Free Launch And Future Fees

## Purpose

This document protects the starting business model for Kaze.

Kaze should launch as a free event, venue, and event-linked services marketplace wherever possible. The goal is to make the platform useful enough that customers, organizers, venue owners, and service providers benefit from easier operations without Kaze adding another early cost layer.

## Free At The Start

At launch:
- guests and attendees should not pay Kaze to use the app
- organizers should not pay Kaze just to coordinate events
- venue owners and managers should be able to list, show, and rent their places without Kaze commission
- event-linked service providers should be able to register and sell services without an early Kaze marketplace cut
- customers should not pay more just because Kaze helped them find a venue or service

This free model helps Kaze build trust, supply, usage, and real demand data.

## Why Free Matters

Kaze should win because it makes event work easier:
- customers find venues and services with less confusion
- venues receive more direct demand
- organizers coordinate invitations, passes, reservations, and services in one place
- service providers meet customers where the event already happens

If Kaze adds cost too early, it risks becoming another middle layer instead of the tool that removes friction.

## How Kaze Can Make Money Later

Kaze can earn later by learning which services sell best, then building or operating those categories directly.

Possible future Kaze-owned or Kaze-operated services:
- photography and videography
- live streaming
- transport
- printing and branding
- decor, flowers, and styling
- wedding and ceremony services
- managed corporate event packages

This keeps the platform open to competition while allowing Kaze to participate in the strongest categories.

## Payment Provider Costs

Payment rail costs are different from Kaze commissions.

If a payment provider charges a fee, Kaze may need to show or pass through that fee transparently. Examples include:
- Apple Pay
- MTN MoMo
- Airtel Money
- IremboPay
- BK / RSwitch
- card, bank, or mobile-money alternatives in other countries

These should be explained as payment-provider costs, not hidden platform markups.

## Future Review Rule

Before adding any Kaze fee, ask:

Does this fee make customers, venues, organizers, or service providers worse off than the manual market Kaze is trying to improve?

If yes, the fee should wait.

The preferred path is to keep core marketplace access free and let Kaze earn through owned services, optional managed packages, and transparent payment rail handling.
